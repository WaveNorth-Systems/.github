# Code of Conduct

Wave North Systems is committed to a professional, respectful, and harassment-free working environment.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Focus feedback on the work and its effect.
- Welcome useful questions and different levels of experience.
- Protect confidential company, customer, and contributor information.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to an organization owner through the established company channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as circumstances allow. Organization owners may remove content, restrict access, or take other proportionate action.
